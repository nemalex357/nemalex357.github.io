Many screenshots — one picture

Pick the screenshots of a conversation. StitchChat joins them into one tall picture, without repeats and without the messenger's bars.

  • Stitched, not pasted. Overlaps are found by the messages themselves; the seam is shown to you if the app is not sure.
  • Personal details, one tap away. Tap "Hide personal details" and names, avatars, phone numbers, emails, @usernames, links and faces are outlined in pink. Hide them by group or all at once, with opaque pink patches.
  • Nothing leaves your phone. No servers, no accounts, no analytics. We cannot see your screenshots, because they never leave your iPhone.
